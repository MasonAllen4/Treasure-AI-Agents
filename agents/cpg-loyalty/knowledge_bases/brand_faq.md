# Brand & Product FAQ

## About Our Products

**Are your products gluten-free?**
Our core snack line is certified gluten-free. Check individual product packaging for allergen information. Our baked goods line is produced in a facility that also processes wheat.

**What are your sustainability commitments?**
We use 100% recyclable packaging across our core range as of 2025. We are committed to reducing Scope 3 emissions by 30% by 2028 and source ingredients from certified sustainable farms.

**Where are your products available?**
Available at major grocery retailers nationwide including Walmart, Target, Kroger, and Whole Foods. Use our store locator at brand.com/find for local availability.

## Loyalty Program

**How do I earn points?**
- 1 point per $1 spent on brand.com
- 2x points during double-point events (announced via email/app)
- Bonus points for product reviews (50 pts), referrals (100 pts), and social shares (25 pts)

**When do points expire?**
Points expire 12 months after the date they were earned if your account is inactive (no purchase or engagement).

**How do I redeem points?**
Log in at brand.com/rewards or ask me to show your available rewards. Points can be redeemed for discounts, free products, or charitable donations.
