# Subscriber FAQ — Media Streaming Platform

## Account & Billing

**How do I update my payment method?**
Go to Account Settings > Billing > Update Payment. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

**Can I pause my subscription?**
Yes. You can pause for up to 3 months from Account Settings > Manage Plan > Pause. Your watchlist and history are saved.

**What happens when my subscription expires?**
Your access ends at midnight on the expiry date. Your profile and watchlist are retained for 90 days if you choose to reactivate.

**Do you offer a discount if I stay?**
Loyalty offers are available for subscribers on Standard or Premium plans who have been active for 12+ months. Contact billing support to check eligibility.

## Content & Streaming

**Why can't I find a specific title?**
Availability varies by region and licensing. Use the search function to check current availability. New titles are added weekly.

**What's the difference between Premium and Standard?**
Premium: 4K UHD, Dolby Atmos, 4 simultaneous streams, offline downloads.
Standard: HD 1080p, 2 simultaneous streams, no offline downloads.

**How do I download content for offline viewing?**
Available on Premium plan only. Tap the download icon on any title. Downloads are stored for 30 days.

## Re-engagement

**I haven't watched in a while — where do I start?**
We recommend checking your "Continue Watching" row and the "New for You" section, which is updated based on your viewing history.

**How do content recommendations work?**
Recommendations are generated from your watch history, ratings, search behavior, and genre preferences. The more you watch, the more accurate they become.
